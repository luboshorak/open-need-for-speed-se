# OpenNFS1 SE Reloaded — Credits and provenance

## Current fork
**Current redevelopment, project maintenance and integration: Luboš Horák**

This fork modernizes OpenNFS1 for contemporary .NET and MonoGame, and expands it with restored NFS SE gameplay, original-data import, menus, audio, collisions, vehicle presentations and tournament saving. The changes are recorded in the Git history and pull requests; these credits are not intended to erase any earlier contributions.

## Earlier OpenNFS1 foundations
**Jeff Harris / 1am Studios** — author of the original C#/XNA and MonoGame OpenNFS1 project, including its foundational engine, rendering, original-data integration, and initial game systems. Original repository: https://github.com/jeff-1amstudios/OpenNFS1

Original source-format research also drew on work by **Ian Brown** and **Denis Auroux**, as acknowledged by the upstream project and by parsing-source comments.

The original project's Git history and source attributions remain part of the fork. No claim is made that the present redevelopment independently authored the entire earlier codebase.

## The original game and its creators
**Electronic Arts, Pioneer Productions / EA Seattle** and other respective rights holders retain rights in the original *Road & Track Presents: The Need for Speed* and *Need for Speed Special Edition* game assets, recordings and software.

The game data is loaded from the player's own legally obtained copy, and is not shipped with this repository. This is a fan redevelopment, not an EA release or endorsement.

## Third-party libraries
OpenNFS1 SE uses .NET, MonoGame, DiscUtils and other dependencies, subject to their own terms. The source repository currently has no separately identified root license file; contact the relevant rights holders before assuming further redistribution permissions.

## Original audio research
ItsDanik / ReNFS supplied factual research into original sound slots, bank metadata and race events used by the independent C# audio repair: https://github.com/ItsDanik/ReNFS . See docs/mappings/ingame-audio.md for evidence and limits.
